# Docee branding and app direction

The current mockup uses the sketch direction in [art direction](art-direction.md); an earlier revision followed a blue and beige reference. The observations below record Docee's original public branding.

Researched 7 October 2026. Primary source: [Docee's Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/docee.co/?hl=en). The public profile, bio and twelve visible recent grid tiles were inspected in the browser. Full post captions and further posts required an Instagram login and were not inspected. [Captured profile](evidence/docee-instagram.png).

## Observed brand

- The profile uses a smiling line-art berry mascot with leaves, drawn in vivid blue on a light background. The actual profile image is included as `public/assets/docee-logo.jpg`.
- The displayed name is `DOCEE.`. Cups and staff uniforms show a softer lowercase `docee.` wordmark. The prototype pairs the real mascot with a lowercase type treatment; this text is an approximation, not a supplied master wordmark.
- Royal blue uniforms, blue accents, white surfaces, stainless steel and clear branded cups recur across the visible grid. The food adds berry pink, matcha green, caramel, cream and chocolate colour.
- The bio explicitly names açaí, smoothies, coffee and viral desserts. This is a café and dessert brand with broader menu coverage than a frozen-yoghurt-only app.
- Recent grid tiles show preparation, staff, customers, drinks and dessert close-ups. The accessible public feed feels lively, personal and product-driven. This is an interpretation of the observed visuals, not a published brand guideline.
- The bio lists Findon Fair, Shop 3/115 Findon Road, Woodville South, SA, and Firle Plaza, Shop 9/171 Glynburn Road, Firle, SA. These two venues inform the store screens. Current hours and available menu items still need merchant confirmation.

## Translation into the app

Use cobalt as the main identity colour, generous pale space and dark readable text. The proposed UI colours are approximately `#2425D6` cobalt, `#FAF9F5` cream, `#171720` ink and restrained food-inspired supporting colours. These are design choices derived from the observed brand, not official colour specifications.

Use the actual berry mascot for welcome, membership and reward moments. Reserve rounded serif type for the `docee.` treatment and selected short headlines, with a clear sans-serif for forms, points and navigation. Show real brand imagery rather than generic dessert stock photography. The app's açaí, Ocean Boost Smoothie, iced matcha and Kinder cream photos come from Docee's merchant listing; [product provenance](product-asset-sources.md) distinguishes delivery listing prices from unverified in-store prices.

The voice should be friendly and brief: “A little more sweet”, “Your next treat”, “Scan. Sip. Repeat.” Keep the return-visit loop visible: balance, progress, scan card, earned reward and one seasonal discovery. Avoid making customers hunt through a campaign feed to find their membership card.

## Competitor implication

[Yo-Chi research](yochi-research.md) confirms a loyalty card, offers, store discovery, receipt claims and loyalty history. Its current listing uses Home, Account, Offers and More navigation. Docee can adopt the practical coverage while expressing its own blue mascot-led identity and broader café menu.

The proposed Docee navigation prioritises Home, Rewards, Scan, Discover and Account. Store discovery and receipt recovery remain easy to reach. The research view contains the real Yo-Chi logo for comparison; customer-facing Docee screens use only Docee branding.

## What is proposed, not verified

“Docee Club”, its points programme, member names, balances, rewards, birthdays, campaigns, prices and transaction examples are concept data. There is no verified existing Docee loyalty scheme in the inspected material. The mockup proposes one point per dollar, with sample $5 credit at 100 points, a coffee at 120 points and a bowl at 250 points. Economics, eligibility, expiry, reward limits and POS behaviour must be agreed before implementation.

The prototype sends no OTP, creates no real account and issues no valid voucher. It supports review of the interface and navigation. Instagram assets and merchant product photographs are preserved unaltered; their URLs are recorded in [asset provenance](docee-asset-sources.json) and [product provenance](product-asset-sources.md).
