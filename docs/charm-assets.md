# Docee charm assets

Five original, transparent SVG charms add a small pen-drawn accent to the Treat Club direction. Each uses only the supplied mascot's vivid blue, `#0000FB`, on a 100 × 100 viewBox. Slightly uneven curves, round ends, and varied 2.5–3.6-unit strokes keep the family expressive without adding visual noise.

| Asset | Intended use |
| --- | --- |
| `public/assets/charms/berry.svg` | A strawberry with a loose leaf crown and tiny seed marks; a flavour charm, without a face or feet. |
| `public/assets/charms/spoon-heart.svg` | A tilted spoon paired with a small heart; reward and little-treat moments. |
| `public/assets/charms/iced-drink.svg` | An iced cup, straw, and loose ice details; drinks and menu discovery. |
| `public/assets/charms/acai-swirl.svg` | A soft swirl in a shallow bowl; açaí and reward details. |
| `public/assets/charms/sparkles.svg` | An asymmetric sparkle cluster; a restrained confirmation or ticket accent. |

Use at roughly 40–80 CSS pixels, with generous pale space. One charm near a heading or ticket is enough; keep the real products and useful membership controls dominant. The broad silhouettes remain clear at small sizes, while the smaller strokes add personality at larger sizes.

These are newly drawn vector motifs, not stock icons and not a redraw or modification of the authentic Docee mascot. They contain no visible text, fonts, external resources, background panels, or embedded images. The original mascot and merchant photographs remain unchanged. The berry deliberately omits the mascot's face, feet, and distinctive leafy head construction.
